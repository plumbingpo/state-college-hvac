Safety

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. We inspect heat exchangers for cracks, confirm correct venting and combustion air, check electrical connections and disconnects, document the refrigerant charge, protect against condensate overflow, and measure ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is simple: protecting the people in your building from CO hazards, poor air quality, and water damage. Findings go into a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

The paperwork matters as much as the repair. Every account gets a service binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, is put on a more frequent visit schedule.

State College HVAC offers fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout State College and surrounding areas, with an experienced technician dispatched as soon as you call.
